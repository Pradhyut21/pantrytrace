# Brag plan: PantryTrace

The app reconciles synthetic food-bank recall, stock, receipt and transfer records into evidence-linked internal task drafts.

## Planning rubric

1. App: a recall investigation workbench for fragmented pantry records.
2. Strongest claim: missing evidence becomes a question to investigate.
3. Visual hook: a huge missing lot symbol beside an ordinary stock record; forest-green ink on warm paper.
4. Actual UI: scenario selection, resulting stock decision register, evaluator trace and evidence panel.
5. Shortest satisfying version: 24 seconds.
6. Tone: polished; a calm operational investigation with a sharp first line.
7. Audio: restrained instrumental bed and two quiet UI accents, no narration.
8. Caption: “PantryTrace connects a recall notice to fragmented pantry records, follows the evidence, and drafts internal tasks for human review. Synthetic prototype; sponsor integrations still require live verification.”
9. User flow: choose missing-lot scenario → investigate → inspect retrieved receipt and revised decision; contrast a conflicting-record case.

## Angle and visual identity

Hook: “The recall is clear. Your records aren't.” The film moves from an unanswered lot field into the actual workbench, then ends on the principle “Evidence before action.” It shows prototype behavior without claiming production readiness or health outcomes.

Landscape, 1920×1080, 24 seconds. Paper #f6f6ef, forest #174b3b, ink #203c33, lime #dce9a5, orange #aa542c. Display font DM Serif Display; body DM Sans, bundled with OFL licenses. Product captures retain their own UI typography.
