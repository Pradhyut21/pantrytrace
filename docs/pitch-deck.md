## 2. A notice starts the investigation

The coordinator still has to inspect stock, find the lot records and identify
partner transfers. The notice provides product identity and affected lots; stock
can contain a supplier alias and missing lot; transfer records provide the partner
location and quantity moved.

Partner-agency guidance requires inventory inspection and quarantine of recalled
product.

Source: Foodbank of Southeastern Virginia and the Eastern Shore, Partner Agency
Manual, January 2026, recall section:
https://foodbankonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PAM-Jan-2026.pdf

## 4. The critic changes the plan

1. Propose from available stock records.
2. Critic identifies the missing lot.
3. Query the supplier alias and exact intake batch.
4. Recompute, or retain human review.

Bounded deterministic roles share evidence and retry. The current controller does
not claim LLM autonomy. The screenshot is an actual local execution trace.

Research pattern references, not reproduced methods or borrowed performance:
ReAct https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629 and Self-RAG

## 5. Conflicting receipts stay unresolved

**18 units require human inspection. 10 units match an affected lot.**

Two receipts disagree about the same batch. The system keeps both records and
requests a physical label check. Every operational action remains an internal
draft for a person to approve.

Proof: synthetic conflict scenario INV-C01, LEDGER-C01 and LEDGER-C02; INV-C02
matches lot NS260902. No food-safety clearance or public alert is produced.
