# Media credits and provenance

These credits apply to [the 116-second walkthrough](pantrytrace-walkthrough.mp4), [the 24-second teaser](pantrytrace-brag.mp4) and their posters. The assets were prepared on **19–20 October 2026**, before the official build window. Their operational records are synthetic, and their footage represents the local prototype rather than verified live sponsor execution.

- Music: **Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 12** by **Sascha Ende**, [official track](https://ende.app/en/song/12881-happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12), [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://ende.app/standard-license). The source was bundled with the installed brag workflow. The videos trim the track, lower its gain and add entry/end fades. The original track page was checked on 19 October 2026. Retain this attribution when posting.
- Sound effects: **Kenney**, [official source](https://kenney.nl/). Bundled `interface/bong_001.ogg` and `interface/click_003.ogg` were used as quiet interface/reveal accents in the teaser.
- Fonts: **DM Serif Display** and **DM Sans** from Google Fonts. The original composition's SIL Open Font License texts are retained here as [display-font license](licenses/OFL-display.txt) and [body-font license](licenses/OFL-body.txt). The published media contains rendered text; font binaries are not included in this folder.
- Rendering runtime: the original compositions used **GSAP 3.14.2** and HyperFrames. The runtime and editable composition projects are not redistributed in this media folder.
- App footage and screenshots: captured from the actual local PantryTrace workbench, using a 1440×900 viewport. The operational data is synthetic. Screenshots retain the application's real result, evidence and trace content.
- Verification images: styled transcripts of captured local test output and development evaluation, action-packet and Meterless smoke output. They are not screenshots of a separate terminal application. See the repository's [validation notes](../validation.md) for the documented checks and their limits.

No stock footage, generated product mockups or voiceover was used in producing these videos. Product footage received no color treatment.
